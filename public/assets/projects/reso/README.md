# Reso case-study assets

Ten selected figures for `/projects/reso`, extracted from the user-supplied
`Grey Clean Minimalist Professional Project Presentation.pdf` in the local
`Desktop/个人网站/VR-Deaf` folder. Sources remain unmodified. Final public
materials are listed below; no raw participant logs or interview material are included.

Images were extracted directly from embedded PDF image objects, converted to RGB,
resized to fit within 1600 × 1400 without enlargement, and encoded as WebP
(quality 85). No generative edits, recolouring, or screenshot reconstruction.

The source interface examples use different utterances; captions explicitly avoid
presenting them as matched trials. The people pictured are in stimulus/interface
examples supplied in the project presentation, not newly published participant data.
Colours inside research evidence are preserved rather than restyled to match the site.
All ten images can be opened at their exported resolution through native links.

Content authority: the user's confirmed case-study brief and contribution statement.
`WinterSchool2026_DHH.pdf` was used to cross-check the prototype and study context.
The source presentation's stronger accessibility claims are deliberately not repeated.
The page distinguishes team findings from Peiwen's contribution and does not claim
that colour alone caused the accuracy difference, that DHH benefit was proven, or
that the post-study revision was evaluated.

Missing evidence: DP1 AR headset mockup / Wizard-of-Oz photographs; verified rhythm
graph before/after screenshots. Exact DP1/DP2 month ranges remain unconfirmed.
